Geographical Information Update

To enhance the analytical capabilities of VPD-SMART, accurate and granular geographical boundaries for Level 0 (country), Level 1 (region), and Level 2 (district) administrative divisions (ADM) are available.

Example: Level 0 & Level 1 boundaries visualized in VPD-SMART dashboards

Integration of GIS_CODE for spatial analysis and reporting

Spatial Analysis & GIS Integration

The integration of geographical information using the GML format and the PAHO-created GIS_CODE enables accurate visualization and spatial analysis of VPD data. This spatial analysis capacity is crucial for informing targeted public health interventions and policies, and for facilitating spatially oriented research on VPD distribution and determinants, thereby directly impacting the system's utility for policy and research.

Simple 3-Step Installation Process

Get your surveillance system running in under 30 minutes with our step-by-step guide and automated installation tools.

1

Download Package

Select your desired surveillance package (AFP or MR) and download the complete metadata bundle with documentation.

wget https://github.com/im-data-paho/vpd-smart/releases/latest/vpd-package.zip
2

Import & Configure

Use our automated import script to install metadata into your DHIS2 instance with country-specific configurations.

./install-vpd-smart.sh --country=PER --package=afp
3

Train & Go Live

Access our training materials and support resources to onboard your team and start collecting surveillance data.
